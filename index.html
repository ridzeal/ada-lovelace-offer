The problem

They have a problem now, and "call back during business hours" doesn't work for them. If you don't reach them in the first five minutes, the job goes to whoever answers.

  • After-hours and on-the-tools calls go straight to voicemail — and stay there.

How it works

Three steps, fully automated.

1

Triage

A missed call triggers an instant SMS text-back. Every reply is classified — qualified, needs info, not interested, out of office, or spam — so real buyers rise to the top.

2

Qualify

The AI extracts budget, need and timeline from the conversation and asks one short follow-up for anything missing. Only leads that pass your gates move forward.

3

Book

Qualified leads get a 15-minute slot on your calendar — conflict-free, business hours only. You show up to a call with a pre-qualified buyer.
